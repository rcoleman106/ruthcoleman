Conservation at the Botanical Gardens

A short documentary on endangered and rare plant species, filmed at the National Botanic Gardens in Glasnevin. Our aim was to show what conservation work actually looks like day to day, and what an ordinary visitor can do to help, rather than to explain the problem at people.

  • Role Sound engineer, boom operator
  • Team Three-person group
  • Format Short documentary
  • Location National Botanic Gardens, Glasnevin

The film was built around one question: can botanic gardens actually help solve this? The first act sets out biodiversity loss, habitat destruction and climate change. The second answers it with the gardens themselves, as a place of hope and of active conservation.

01

The brief

Spread awareness of endangered and rare plant species, and show how the Botanic Gardens are helping them survive.

02

Access

Wrote to the Office of Public Works and the gardens' staff for filming permission, and secured interviews with a garden scientist and a member of staff.

03

Filming

All three of us filmed the scenery shots, then chose the strongest takes together. We filmed tours and walks through the gardens alongside the interviews.

04

Sound

I recorded the location sound and boomed the interviews, keeping wind and handling noise off the dialogue and capturing clean ambience for the cutaways.

05

Natural sound

We used gentle, nature-inspired music and recorded clean ambient sound throughout: birds, footsteps, the glasshouses. Some scenes carry no music at all and rely on the surroundings, which gives the film a sense of place that a score would have flattened.

06

Two interviews, handled differently

The scientist appears on camera, so the film can show who is speaking. The staff member is heard over footage of the gardens instead, which keeps the film moving and lets the visuals carry the point she's making. Both were asked the same set of questions, about what the gardens do, what happens to a species if it isn't protected, and what visitors can do to help.

07

Influences

We took our cue from Our Planet, particularly its use of the surroundings as atmosphere rather than decoration. The intent was to give context to what is being discussed, not to make something purely visual.

What I'd do differently

The working files for this project weren't kept, so there's no process record here the way there is for the brochure. On the next project I'd keep a folder of screenshots and exports as I go. It takes seconds at the time and it's the difference between being able to show the work and only being able to describe it.

Outcome

A finished short documentary that makes the case for plant conservation through the people doing the work. My part was the sound: clean dialogue in a live, uncontrolled environment, and ambience that lets the gardens speak for themselves.
